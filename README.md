# 🏨 Revenue-Insights-in-Hospitality-Domain Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Hospitality Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze hotel performance across multiple business dimensions such as Revenue, Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, Realisation, and Revenue Leakage.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators, compare hotel performance, identify revenue loss, and make data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to help hotel management:

**Monitor overall business performance**
**Analyze occupancy and revenue trends**
**Compare hotel performance across cities and properties**
**Identify revenue leakage due to cancellations and no-shows**
**Evaluate booking platform performance**

🆕 Additional Business Insights

Based on feedback from my mentor, I extended the original dashboard by adding two new visuals to improve business decision-making.

1️⃣ Revenue Leakage by Booking Platform

Business Problem Solved

Identifies which booking platforms contribute the most to revenue loss.

Business Value

Highlights platforms with higher cancellation-related losses.
Helps management optimize platform partnerships and marketing strategies.
Makes revenue leakage easier to identify at a glance.
2️⃣ Revenue Leakage by Booking Status

Business Problem Solved

Shows how revenue loss is distributed across booking statuses such as:

Cancelled
Checked Out
No Show

Business Value

Quickly identifies the primary source of lost revenue.
Helps stakeholders understand the impact of cancellations and no-shows.
Supports better operational and pricing decisions.
